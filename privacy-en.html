Privacy Policy

SFL Farmhand · Last updated: October 12, 2026

At SFL Farmhand your privacy comes first. The app has no accounts, no servers of its own and collects no personal data. Purchases of SFL Farmhand Pro are handled by the app store, not by us. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it is stored and who it communicates with.

1. Controller

SFL Farmhand is an independent app developed by Sebastian Hernandez. For any privacy question you can write to nesstrikeonline@gmail.com.

2. Data we don't collect

We don't ask for your name, email, phone, location, contacts, photos or any other personal data. There is no sign-up or login. We never see or store your payment details (card, bank account or billing address). We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and we don't sell or share data with third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Information the app uses

• Farm ID: the public number of your Sunflower Land farm that you enter at the start. It is only used to look up that farm's public data. • Farm data: timers, inventory, level and other public information returned by the Sunflower Land API for that Farm ID. • Preferences: app settings (currency, notifications, home order, daily plan, etc.). • SFL Farmhand Pro status: whether you have the free version, the free trial, the quarterly subscription or the one-time payment plan, and the renewal date. It comes from the app store to unlock features. • Temporary copies (cache): market prices, radar history and game images, so the app runs faster.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

In-app purchases (the quarterly subscription, its free trial and the one-time payment plan) are processed entirely through Apple's App Store or Google Play, with the account signed in on your device. Apple or Google handle payment and billing data according to their own privacy policies. If you redeem an offer code, the redemption is also handled by Apple. The app only receives from the store a signed confirmation of the purchase or redemption (which plan it is, when it started and when it renews or expires). That confirmation is checked on your own device to unlock SFL Farmhand Pro and is not sent to any developer server. The developer can see aggregated, anonymous sales reports in the Apple and Google dashboards, but not your identity or payment details.

5. Where it is stored

All information is stored only on your device: preferences, the last known SFL Farmhand Pro status and the last copy of your farm in the app's storage, the API key in the system Keychain, and caches in the app's folder. None of this is sent to developer servers, because there are none.

6. External services the app uses

To show you up-to-date data, the app connects over HTTPS to these services: • api.sunflower-land.com: farm, marketplace and faction pet data. • sfl.world: community prices and yield data. • api.coingecko.com: FLOWER token price. • sunflower-land.com, animations.sunflower-land.com, pets.sunflower-land.com and raw.githubusercontent.com: game images. • App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google): plans, prices, purchases and purchase restoration. As with any internet connection, these services receive your IP address and the request made (for example, the Farm ID looked up). Each service handles that information according to its own privacy policy; the developer has no access to it.

7. Notifications

"Harvest ready" alerts are local notifications scheduled by your own device. They are only enabled if you grant permission, and you can turn them off at any time in the app's or the system's Settings. The app does not send notifications or promotional alerts to sell you SFL Farmhand Pro.

8. How long it is kept and how to delete it

Information stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete it at any time from Settings → Legal & privacy → "Erase all app data", or by uninstalling the app. Erasing the data does not cancel a subscription or remove your purchases: they remain linked to your Apple or Google account and you can recover them with "Restore purchases".

9. Security

All connections use encrypted HTTPS, the API key is stored in the system Keychain and purchases are verified with the store's cryptographic signature. Even so, no system is 100% foolproof.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from children. Purchases by minors are subject to Apple's parental controls (Family Sharing, "Ask to Buy") or Google's (Family Link).

11. Your rights

Since the app collects no personal data, we have no information about you to access, correct or delete. Everything the app stores is on your device and you can delete it yourself. For data about your purchases, contact Apple or Google. If you have any questions, write to us at nesstrikeonline@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version in the app and on its website, with the update date.

SFL Farmhand is an unofficial app and is not affiliated with Sunflower Land. Contact: nesstrikeonline@gmail.com
