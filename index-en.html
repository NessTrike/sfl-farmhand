Your farm helper for Sunflower Land

Live timers for your farm, profitability in FLOWER, market radar, Marine Marvels guides, pets, foods to level up and much more.

FAQ

Where do I find my Farm ID? It's the number next to your name in the game.

Does the app need my wallet or password? No. It only uses your public Farm ID; it will never ask for private keys or recovery phrases.

Why doesn't a value match the game? Some values are estimates and the data depends on external services. Pull down to refresh.

How do I delete my data? In Settings → Legal & privacy → "Erase all app data", or by uninstalling the app. Your purchases are not lost.

How do I change the language? In Settings → Language (or the ES | EN switch on the first screen).

SFL Farmhand Pro

What does it include? Activity, Market radar, Marvels progress, Foods to level up, Animal profitability, Pets, Available digs and Faction pet. The rest of the app is free.

How much is it? A quarterly subscription of 1.99 USD every 3 months (with 7 days free the first time) or a one-time payment of 9.99 USD with no renewals. The store shows the final price in your currency.

How do I cancel the subscription? iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period (or of the trial) so it doesn't renew.

How do I request a refund? Payments are handled by the store: at reportaproblem.apple.com (App Store) or in your Google Play purchase history.

I changed phones, do I lose Pro? No. Sign in with the same Apple or Google account and tap "Restore purchases" in Settings → SFL Farmhand Pro.

I have a code. Tap "Redeem code" in Settings → SFL Farmhand Pro or in the plans sheet.
